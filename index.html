The Weekly Dispatch
Vol. II, No. 1. Monday, 28 September MMXXVI

On the Tetractys

This week we returned to the tetractys, ten points arranged in four rows, and found that it had more to say than we remembered. We counted the rows, then the sums, then asked why anyone should have found this small triangle worth swearing an oath upon.

No answer was reached. A page of constructions was drawn, one was spoiled, and a second was made more slowly and came out better. A French book was begun and set aside after three pages, on the understanding that it would be taken up again.

Colophon

The King's Station Society of Inquiry is a small private society kept by one family. It keeps a record of what is read, reckoned, played, made, and wondered about, and it is published a little after the fact.

Visitors are welcome to write to the Dean or to ask for a game of chess.
